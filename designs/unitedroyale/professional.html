Our Story

Two decades of building trusted partnerships

Incorporated in 2000, United Royale Corporation is a licensed outsourcing service provider, duly registered with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) under Department Order No. 174, Series of 2017 (Permit No. RO1-09-23-00021). With solid years of understanding the industry, we deliver end-to-end service solutions — from sourcing and screening highly competent personnel to deploying technical, administrative, and clerical support staff across a broad spectrum of business and industries.

Backed by a paid-up capital of ₱10 million, our foundation gives partners a service provider they can rely on year-round. In URC, we provide solutions — we don't just develop clients, we nurture partnerships.
